# Apple Storage Conditions

Apple has the longest storage potential of nearly all fresh fruits, largely
because it is normally harvested mature but not yet fully ripe (the
"preclimacteric" stage), and most cultivars have a comparatively low
respiration rate.

Storage temperature recommendations vary by cultivar chilling-sensitivity:
- Chilling-sensitive cultivars: generally held around 0-1°C
- Less chilling-sensitive cultivars: can be held somewhat warmer, >1°C

As a commonly cited reference point, Golden Delicious apples show storage
potential of roughly 6 months in standard refrigerated air storage, extending
to roughly 10 months under controlled atmosphere (CA), when held near 0°C ±1°C
at 90-95% relative humidity.

Some cultivars (e.g. Granny Smith) can experience chilling/low-temperature
injury even at 0°C in some seasons - cultivar-specific behavior matters more
for apples than for many other fruits, and generic thresholds should be treated
as approximate.

Relative humidity of 90-95% is generally recommended to limit moisture loss
without promoting decay.
