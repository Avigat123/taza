# Banana Storage Conditions

Recommended holding temperature for green/mature-green bananas is approximately
13-14°C (55-58°F). This range slows respiration and ripening without triggering
chilling injury. At this temperature, properly stored green bananas can typically
be held for up to about three weeks, compared to only a few days at uncontrolled
tropical ambient temperatures.

Ripening rooms (once ripening is intentionally triggered with ethylene) run
warmer, approximately 14-20°C (58-68°F).

Relative humidity recommendations:
- Green or turning fruit: 90-95% RH
- Ripe fruit: 85-90% RH

Humidity below this range causes moisture loss through the peel, leading to
shriveling and accelerated deterioration. Humidity above roughly 95% encourages
fungal growth, so precise control matters in both directions.

Under commercial cold-chain management (temperature and humidity both
controlled), overall banana storage life is commonly cited in the range of
4-8 weeks, though this depends heavily on cultivar and how promptly the fruit
was cooled after harvest (precooling).

Source note: this is a produce-fact-sheet summary from a university postharvest
extension center, not a peer-reviewed primary study; treat cited ranges as
practical guidance rather than exact scientific constants.
