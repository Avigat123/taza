# Orange Ripening Behavior

Unlike banana and (to a lesser extent) apple, citrus fruits including orange
are non-climacteric: they do not continue to ripen significantly after harvest
in the way climacteric fruit does. Quality (flavor, sugar/acid balance) is
therefore largely fixed at harvest and does not meaningfully improve in
storage - storage and cooling primarily slow decay and water loss rather than
"finishing" ripening.

Practical implication: for oranges, a CV-observed visual quality issue is more
likely to reflect decay, chilling injury, or physical damage rather than
over-ripeness in the way it would for banana. Flavor quality is linked to
soluble-solids-to-acid ratio and absence of off-flavor compounds from
fermentative metabolism, which are not directly visible in images.
