# Orange Storage Conditions

Typical recommended storage range is approximately 3-8°C (38-46°F) for up to
about 3 months, though the exact optimum depends heavily on cultivar, maturity
and ripeness stage at harvest, and production region.

Regional/cultivar variation is significant and should be treated as a real
source of uncertainty, not noise:
- Some Florida-grown cultivars tolerate storage as low as 0-1°C
- Arizona-grown Valencia oranges are recommended to be kept warmer, around 9°C,
  reflecting different chilling sensitivity by growing region/cultivar

Mandarins (a related but distinct citrus type, sometimes visually similar to
orange in casual imaging) are more chilling-sensitive than oranges and require
higher minimum storage temperatures, typically 5-8°C, with typical postharvest
life of roughly 3-12 weeks depending on variety and conditions.

Prompt cooling after harvest and consistent maintenance of temperature and
humidity throughout marketing/distribution are emphasized as more important to
final quality than any single storage parameter in isolation.
