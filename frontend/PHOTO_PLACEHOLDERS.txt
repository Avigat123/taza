4. BATCH THUMBNAILS (grid cards + batch detail page)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Files:
  src/components/batches/BatchCard.jsx   (grid view thumbnail)
  src/pages/BatchDetails.jsx             (larger image on the detail page)

Look for: "PHOTO PLACEHOLDER: produce thumbnail"

What to do:
  Each mock batch in src/data/mockData.js has an `imageKey` field
  (e.g. "mango-102"). Right now that key is just displayed as text inside
  a colored box. Once the backend returns a real image URL per batch
  (e.g. `batch.imageUrl`), replace the placeholder <div> with:

    <img src={batch.imageUrl} alt={batch.produce} className="w-full h-28
    object-cover rounded-lg" />

  Until then, you can also drop static demo photos in /public/produce/
  (e.g. /public/produce/mango-102.jpg) and reference them by imageKey as a
  quick stand-in for the demo:

    <img src={`/produce/${batch.imageKey}.jpg`} ... />
